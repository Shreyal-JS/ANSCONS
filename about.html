CLAUSE 01 // ON SUBSTANCE AND SKIN

A Building Must Be Built From What It Claims To Be.

We wage war against simulated elegance. In modern residential construction, drywall is masked with textured plaster, plastic laminate mimics walnut, and thin stone veneers pass as monolithic architecture. "No faux facades — honest stone or nothing." If a wall appears to be stone, it must bear load as authentic stone.

Structure dictates form. Reject all decorative illusions!
CLAUSE 02 // ON THE SANCTITY OF TOLERANCE

The Eye Senses Fractions of a Millimeter.

The human subconscious registers geometric misalignment long before the conscious mind can name it. A reveal that varies by a mere 2mm between header and jamb destroys the tranquil harmony of a room. Hence, our structural tolerance is locked at ±0.5mm or dismantle.

Inspect every joint personally on site before pour! - AC
CLAUSE 03 // ON MULTI-CENTURY PERMANENCE

Architecture Is Frozen Time, Not A Consumable Commodity.

A private residence is the most profound physical container of human memory. A structure that requires total renovation after fifteen years is not luxury; it is engineering delinquency. We build for the grandchildren of our clients.
